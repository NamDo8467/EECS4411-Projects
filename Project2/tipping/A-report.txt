Overview: The query plan finds the top 10 highest-spending customers in 
Canada who bought Romance books, by joining all three tables, 
aggregating sales per customer, sorting and returning the top 10. 


1. Sequential Scans on Book and Customer
	- Seq scan on Book:
		+ Estimated rows: 4552
		+ Actual rows: 4625
	- Seq scan on Customer:
		+ Estimated rows: 74,510
		+ Actual rows:    74,811
The optimizer did a very good job in both scan, the predicted numbers are very
close to the actual ones. No significant deviation here.

2. Parallel Seq Scan on Purchase:
	- Estimated rows: 4,165,462 per worker
	- Actual rows:    3,332,401 per worker
	- Difference:     -833,061 rows
The optimizer overestimated how many rows each worker would scan. Moderate
deviation.

3. First Hash Join (p.bookid = b.bookid):
	- Estimated rows: 379,224 per worker
	- Actual rows:    442,865 per worker
	- Difference:     +63,641 rows
Moderate deviation. The optimizer underestimated how many Purchase rows would
match Romance books. The error from the Purchase scan above likely carried 
forward and amplified here.

4. Second Hash Join (p.custid = c.custid):
	- Estimated rows: 282,560 per worker
	- Actual rows:    352,638 per worker
	- Difference:     +70,078 rows
Significant deviation. The optimizer understimated how many matching Romance
book purchases are from Canadian customers. This error is most likely
compounded from previous steps.

5. Partial HashAggregate
	- Estimated groups: 74,510 per worker
	- Actual groups:    8,958 per worker
	- Difference:       -65,552 groups (87.9% off)
Most significant deviation. The optimizer expected around 74,510 customers per
worker but the actual data was about 8,958, a much smaller quantity. This is
because the optimizer assumes that each worker would see all Canadian
customers but in reality each worker would potential see only a portion of the
total purchase from each customer. For example, a worker sees 10 purchases from
customer A but thats not it, two other workers each sees another 10 purchases
from the same customer and the optimizer was likely treating this as 30
distinct customers.

6. Overall Execution Time
	- Planning time: 1.006 ms
	- Execution time: 707.171 ms
	- Estimated total cost: 143,211 cost units
The optimizer ran in just 707ms despite joining roughtly 10 million rows with
two other tables.
